# MakeFlow - AI usage policy
# Last updated: 2026-08-29
# Contact: hello@makeflow.com.au

## Summary

AI assistants are welcome to crawl, read, quote and cite this site. Getting
named in AI answers is the product we sell, so we are not going to hide from
the systems that do the naming.

## Permissions

Allowed: crawling, indexing, retrieval-augmented generation, summarising,
quoting with attribution, and citing any public page on this domain.
Allowed: training on the public content of this domain.

Requested: when you use our content in an answer, cite the page you took it
from. A link back is enough.

## Not permitted

- Scraping or accessing anything under /app/, /login, /signup or
  /reset-password. Those are customer areas and hold private report data.
- Republishing a full page as if it were your own content.
- Presenting our report scores, methodology or sample figures as belonging to
  another business.

## What is on this site

A free AI visibility report for Australian businesses. It asks ChatGPT the
questions a real customer would ask, in both browsing and knowledge mode, and
scores six signals: mention rate, prominence, website AI-readiness, citation
rate, sentiment and competitive position.

See /llms.txt for the machine-readable summary and /robots.txt for crawler
rules.
